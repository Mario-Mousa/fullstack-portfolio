# Mobile card verification

The narrow portrait reference shows the profile identity card clipping its contact and social-content area at the lower edge. The current fixed desktop rail height can constrain the card on narrow portrait viewports before its natural content height is reached. The responsive fix should let the identity rail and its card expand vertically on narrow portrait screens.

After the responsive update, the narrow portrait rule lets the rail and card extend beyond the initial viewport instead of constraining them to the desktop-height behavior. The contact details and social links remain present in the rendered page structure and can flow below the fold.
